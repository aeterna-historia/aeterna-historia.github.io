Qué hace

  • Sube y programa los vídeos del canal en YouTube, con su título, su descripción y su miniatura.

Qué permisos pide a Google

  • En YouTube: gestionar los vídeos del canal.
  • En Google Drive: ver, crear, cambiar y borrar los ficheros de ese Drive.

Solo sobre las cuentas del canal, y solo cuando su titular da el permiso en la pantalla de Google.

English

What it does

Aeterna subida is the private tool the YouTube channel Aeterna Historia uses to do two jobs:

  • Upload and schedule the channel's videos on YouTube, with their title, description and thumbnail.
  • Store and read the channel's production files in its own Google Drive, from the server where the work is done.

Who uses it

Only the channel's owner. It is not open to the public, has no sign-up and offers no service to anyone else.

Google permissions it asks for

  • On YouTube: manage the channel's videos.
  • On Google Drive: see, create, edit and delete the files in that Drive.

Only on the channel's own accounts, and only when their owner grants access on Google's consent screen.
